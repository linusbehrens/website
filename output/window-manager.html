What I expect from my dream window manager:

  • Open source: Minimal requirement is public code.
  • NO ANIMATIONS: Looking at you hyprland.
  • Minimal design: I want nothing to distract me and the maximal space for the content.
  • Strong key-maps: M-1 for switching to tab 1 and custom key-maps for apps.
  • On wayland: Because of high-DPI screens and x11 scaling.
  • Active software support: Looking at you dwl.

Window managers I know and critizise for:

  • dwm: no wayland…
  • oxwm: no wayland…
  • dwl: no support…
  • hyprland: ANIMATIONS! and bad design
  • niri: No strong key-maps

If you have any other window managers passing my requirements, please mail me.
